How lessons work

After the trial lesson, you get a written plan for the student.

The plan covers

  • Level and skills to work on
  • Goals, test dates and target scores
  • The school's textbooks and exam dates
  • Materials: school books, my handouts, or books to buy
  • Checkpoints after each exam

A weekly lesson (2 hours)

  • Quiz on last week's most-missed points
  • This week's topic, taught mainly in English
  • Practice, checked on the spot
  • Homework and next week's quiz range

Good to know

  • Notes and homework go into a shared folder after every lesson
  • Cancellations: known in advance, we move the lesson; last-minute, it's postponed and not charged
  • Message me on LINE any time

Questions parents ask

Are lessons taught in English?

Mostly, yes. Bilingual and international-school students are taught entirely in English, including Math, Social Studies and Science. For IELTS, TOEFL and other tests, speaking and writing are entirely in English. For students at local Taiwanese schools, grammar may be explained in Chinese so the concepts are precise. We agree on the balance at the trial lesson.

Is there a trial lesson?

Yes. One hour, charged at the regular rate and paid after the lesson. Within two days you'll receive material suggestions, a course plan and a shared folder.

What should I include in my first message?

The student's grade and school, your district (or "online"), and the goal: school, a test or a target score.

Can two students take lessons together?

Yes. Pairs are charged per student at a lower rate. If the two students are at different levels, we'll discuss the rate.

What if we need to cancel or reschedule?

If you know in advance, we'll move the lesson to another time. A last-minute cancellation is postponed and not charged; if an exam is close, we'll find a time for a make-up lesson.

What if the trial lesson isn't a good fit?

Just tell me. There will be no follow-up messages.
