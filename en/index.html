PART 3How I Teach

How lessons work

✓A written course plan with short-, mid- and long-term goals, shared with parents in Google Sheets
✓Handouts I write myself, adjusted to the student's school and test
✓Grammar rules explained clearly, with fully worked example sentences
✓Analogies and visuals to make hard points easier to understand
✓After every test, a look at why each mistake happened, and a change of plan if needed
✓A summary of what we covered after every lesson, for review

A course plan for each student, after the trial lesson

Every course can be customized. After the trial lesson, you'll get a written plan for the student, including the materials and the points below.

🧭 What the plan takes into account

  • Current level and the skills to work on (speaking, reading, writing, grammar…)
  • Goals and timing: school exams, target scores and test dates
  • School: textbooks, exam dates and coverage; bilingual and international programs have their own calendars
  • How often and how long lessons are, and what is done in class versus as homework
  • Materials: the school's or your own books, my handouts, or books to buy (with title and link)
  • Checkpoints: school exams or mock tests; after each one, the focus is adjusted

📅 A weekly lesson (2 hours, as an example)

  • Quiz on last week's most-missed words and points
  • This week's topic: grammar, reading or vocabulary, taught mainly in English
  • Practice in class, checked on the spot
  • Homework and next week's quiz range
  • Before exams: full review and a mock test, then a look at every mistake

📌 Good to know

  • After every lesson, notes, homework and quiz ranges go into a shared folder
  • If you can, share the school's exam dates or syllabus (and the program and class for bilingual or international schools), so the plan follows the school calendar
  • Cancellations: the lesson is postponed and not charged; if an exam is close or we've fallen behind, we'll add a make-up lesson. If you know in advance, we can also move it to another time
  • Payment: the trial is charged at the regular rate; after that, per lesson or monthly; books at the receipt price
  • The plan changes with results; feel free to message me on LINE any time
