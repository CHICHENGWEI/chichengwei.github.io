How lessons work

Following the trial lesson, you will receive a written learning plan tailored to the student.

Each plan covers

  • Current level and skills to develop
  • Goals, test dates and target scores
  • The school's textbooks and examination schedule
  • Materials: school textbooks, my original handouts or recommended books
  • Progress reviews after each examination

A typical weekly lesson (2 hours)

  • A short quiz on the previous week's most common errors
  • The week's topic, taught primarily in English
  • Guided practice with immediate feedback
  • Homework and the scope of the next quiz

Policies

  • Lesson notes and homework are uploaded to a shared folder after each lesson
  • One-on-one: with advance notice, lessons are rescheduled; last-minute cancellations are postponed and not charged
  • Small groups: with three days' notice, the student may join another group at the same level; otherwise, a recording, notes and homework are provided, and LINE questions that week are answered within 24 hours. Missed small-group lessons are charged as usual.
  • Questions are welcome on LINE at any time

Frequently asked questions

Are lessons taught in English?

Primarily, yes. Students at bilingual and international schools are taught entirely in English, including Math, Social Studies and Science. For IELTS, TOEFL and other examinations, speaking and writing are taught entirely in English. For students at local Taiwanese schools, grammar may be explained in Chinese to ensure precise understanding. The balance is agreed at the trial lesson.

Is there a trial lesson?

Yes. The trial lesson lasts one hour and is charged at the standard rate, payable after the lesson. Within two days, you will receive recommended materials, a written learning plan and access to a shared folder.

What should I include in my first message?

Please include the student's grade and school, your district (or "online"), and the goal, such as school results, a particular examination or a target score.

Do you offer group lessons?

Lessons are currently one-on-one. Small-group classes for middle and high school students (three to four per group) are in preparation, and you are welcome to register your interest on LINE.

What if we need to cancel or reschedule?

With advance notice, the lesson will be rescheduled. Last-minute cancellations are postponed and not charged. If an examination is approaching, a make-up lesson will be arranged.

What if the trial lesson is not the right fit?

Simply let me know. There will be no further follow-up.
